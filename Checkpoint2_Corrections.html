Unit 2 Checkpoint 2 Quick Reference Guide

VSEPR 3D shapes, Coulomb's Law, ionic naming rules, polyatomic ions, and IMFs.

1. VSEPR 3D Molecular Geometry Chart

Total Domains Bonds / Lone Pairs 3D VSEPR Shape Polarity & Trap Tip
3 3 bonds, 0 LP Trigonal Planar (120°) Central C with C=O double bond + 2 single bonds (e.g., Glycine Cy, CH2O).
4 4 bonds, 0 LP Tetrahedral (109.5°) Nonpolar only if all 4 terminal atoms match (CF4, CH4); CH2Cl2 & CHF3 are Polar!
4 3 bonds, 1 LP Trigonal Pyramidal Polar because the central lone pair breaks 3D symmetry (PF3, NH3, Glycine Nw).
4 2 bonds, 2 LP Bent (~104.5°) Polar (H2O, Oxygen Oz in –OH groups).

2. IMFs & Coulomb's Law

  • IMF Ranking: H-Bonding (H bonded to N, O, F → Ethanol CH3CH2OH) > Dipole-Dipole (polar CH3Cl) > LDFs (nonpolar CH4).
  • Coulomb Step 1 (Charge Wins!): Compare |q1 × q2| first (+2/-2 CaO >> +1/-1 LiF).
  • Coulomb Step 2 (Radius Ties): Smaller ions pack closer when charges match (Cl- < I- → NaCl > NaI).

3. Ionic Naming & Polyatomics

  • No Prefixes in Ionic: Metal + nonmetal = MgCl2 is magnesium chloride (never dichloride).
  • Roman Numerals = Charge: Iron(III) means Fe3+ + 3 Br- → FeBr3 (never Fe3Br!).
  • Core Polyatomics & Reduce: SO42-, CO32-, NO3-, OH-, PO43-, NH4+. Reduce 2:2 → 1:1 (CuSO4).

4. Valence Math, Isomers & Lattices

  • Ion Valence e-: Subtract for (+) cations (NH4+ = 5 + 4 - 1 = 8); add for (-) anions (OH- = 6 + 1 + 1 = 8).
  • Structural Isomers: Identical formula (C4H10 or C2H6O), different connectivity.
  • Crystals & Conductance: Molten/aqueous ions move & conduct; shifting solid layers aligns like charges → shatters!
